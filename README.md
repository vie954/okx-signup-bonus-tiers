## Three rules decide whether you get paid

### 1. Only designated channels qualify, and OKX won't fix it afterwards

The New User Bonus goes to accounts created through a designated promotional channel, meaning a specific partner referral link or an official OKX campaign page. Register on the ordinary signup form, or through a link that isn't on the designated list, and you can deposit and trade exactly as instructed and still get nothing.

The terms are blunt about the aftermath: if the eligibility check confirms your account wasn't registered through a designated channel, OKX is unable to manually grant the bonus. Appeals go through the Rewards Centre, but the outcome for a non-designated account is no.

### 2. Net deposit is deposits minus withdrawals, not deposits

The formula is `Net Deposit = Qualifying Deposits − Qualifying Withdrawals`. Several things never count as a qualifying deposit:

- internal transfers from another OKX user
- transfers between your own accounts or sub-accounts
- failed, reversed, refunded or recalled deposits
- funds from Crypto Gift or another promotional reward

OKX's own example: deposit 10,000, withdraw 2,000, and your net deposit is 8,000. Receive a 1,000 internal transfer from another user and it stays 8,000, not 9,000. Withdrawals cut the number too, so moving money out during the qualification window can drop you below the threshold even after you've hit it.

One more trap: funds moved into OKX Pay reduce your account balance and can affect eligibility.

### 3. Not every trade counts as a trade

OKX excludes specific pairs and order types from Trade tasks:

- **0-fee trading pairs:** USDC/EUR, USDT/USDC, USDC/USDG
- **0 mark-up fee buy, sell or convert transactions:** USDC/USDT, USDC/USDG, USDC/USD, USDG/USD

Volume on those pairs earns nothing toward a tier. If your plan was to farm a €10,000 trading threshold by cycling stablecoins at zero cost, the terms already closed that door. Wash trading and multi-accounting are listed as disqualifying behaviour, with the right to claw back reward value from your account.

## What a €100 X-Perps voucher is actually worth

Less than €100, and in most outcomes less than €20.

OKX's own footnote explains it: a €100 X-Perps voucher opens a €100 position using €20 margin at 5x leverage. You receive only the profit the position generates, not the €20 margin. If the position closes at a loss, no payout is credited. At the campaign's conversion rate of 1 EUR = 1.15 USDC, the voucher displays to users as 115 USDC.

## When the reward becomes withdrawable: 45-day locks and the Hold Amount

Crypto rewards can be locked for up to 45 days. During that window you can trade the coins but not withdraw them; the unlock progress and estimated release date sit in **Rewards > My Rewards**. The Crypto Insiders campaign states the cash equivalent becomes withdrawable after 30 days.

The mechanic that generates most support tickets is the **Hold Amount**. When OKX pays a crypto reward, it freezes a slice of your balance equal to the reward's live market value. That freeze follows the coin's price, not the moment of payout, and it survives conversion.

OKX's example: you receive 0.001 BTC when BTC trades at $100,000, so the reward is worth $100. You sell it and get $100 USD. BTC then climbs to $120,000. The Hold Amount is recalculated as 0.001 × $120,000 = $120. Your available USD is $100, which is less than the freeze, so the entire balance appears locked, including money that came from your own sale. It releases when the holding requirement is met or the reward is reclaimed.

The $200 figure circulating on Reddit comes from individual posts, not from OKX. Those posts describe a sequence: complete KYC with an SSN or ITIN, deposit at least $200 in cash or crypto, buy at least $200 of crypto, wait 10–15 minutes, then hold an average balance of at least $200 for 30 days within a 90-day window before selling or withdrawing.

OKX's terms support the general shape of that without confirming the specifics. What is confirmed: US residents trade on a separate entity with its own terms and fee schedule, not the global platform, and OKX's restricted-location list excludes several jurisdictions outright. Check that your state is supported before you plan around a bonus.
